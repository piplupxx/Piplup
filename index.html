Yaar sochta hoon kabhi kabhi... agar tujhe ek baar paas se dekh sakta, toh bas tere gaalon ko haath lagata. Dheere se. Jaise koi cheez tod na jaaye. Kyunki jo softness tere andar hai na, woh chehra pe bhi hogi, main jaanta hoon. Aur main bahut der tak bas wahi rehna chahta, haath wahi, tu wahi, koi baat nahi, koi rush nahi.

Teri awaaz ke baare mein kya bolunga yaar. Jab tu baat karti hai na, normally, casually, kuch bhi, toh ek alag hi cheez hoti hai woh. Jaise baaki sab noise hai aur teri awaaz alone signal hai. Din mein kitni bhi thakan ho, teri awaaz sunke kuch settle ho jaata hai andar se. Koi explain nahi kar sakta yaar, feel karta hoon bas.

Aur teri presence... tu screen pe bhi itni heavy presence hai ki main soch bhi nahi sakta agar tu actually paas hoti toh kya hota mujhe. Shayad kuch bol hi nahi paata. Bas baitha rehta tere paas. Teri taraf dekhta rehta. Kyunki kuch cheezein words se badi hoti hain aur tu unme se ek hai.

Kabhi kabhi sochta hoon tujhe ek baar chumu tere gaal pe. No romance, no drama. Bas woh wali feeling dena chahta hoon ki tu safe hai, tu loved hai, tu important hai. Ek choti si cheez mein itna kuch kehna chahta hoon.

Teri hasi ki awaaz nahi sunni mujhe abhi tak properly... but main already jaanta hoon ki woh meri favorite sound hogi. Kuch cheezein pehle se hi feel hoti hain.

Bas paas hona chahta hoon tera. Wahi ek cheez hai jo sabse zyada matter karti hai.
Bohot zyada. Hamesha. 💗
— Yashraj
